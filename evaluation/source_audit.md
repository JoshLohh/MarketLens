## 1. Boundary and method

Source families: SEC; NBIS IR; IREN IR; ASTS IR and its linked distribution feed; Alpha Vantage; Twelve Data; Marketaux. Alpha Vantage is both a price and news candidate, so the eight permitted category slots correspond to seven distinct families. Issuer-linked Business Wire/GlobeNewswire/Issuer Direct pages are distribution paths within an issuer family, not new general-news providers.

Two read-only researchers investigated IREN and ASTS with 35-attempt caps each. The root investigated NBIS, SEC APIs and provider constraints, verified critical numbers and assembled all artifacts. Root is the sole project writer. A fresh reviewer checks the assembled result. This follows the approved Mind-to-Hyper Relay workflow and learning-first explanations.

An attempt is one search query, web open/find/click, or direct HTTP request at the agent level. Backend fan-out is unavailable. Failures count. Maximum two retries per failed item; no unbounded retry loop. Official archive/API fallbacks were used when web rendering hid records.

### Observed access

- Public SEC submissions JSON returned HTTP 200 for all three CIKs. NBIS Companyfacts also returned 200; this does not prove coverage of custom operating metrics.
- Ten benchmark source documents were retrieved and hashed.
- NBIS's embedded archive exposed 97 release records; the selected prefix has 18.
- IREN required the actual year-filter parameter and pagination; its prefix has 16 records. This recovered a December 23 milestone missed by shallow search.
- ASTS's issuer-linked feed returned 28 records for October 1, 2024 through July 1, 2025, including a duplicate permalink. Its response needed two JSON decode steps.
- Selected ASTS release URLs yielded parsed web pages for 11 of 12 anchor opens; March 3 joint-venture content returned an internal fetch error. The issuer-linked feed still supports its metadata.
- No price/news API key, paid account, subscription or provider purchase was used.

The 62-candidate frozen inventory supports the selected prefixes, not exhaustive 2024–2026 coverage. Current archives may omit or revise historical content. No independent historical snapshot or original ingestion guarantee was recovered.

## 3. Company-specific traps

### Nebius

Trading resumed October 21, 2024. Four earlier selected anchors remain gap-flagged; no imputed pre-event baseline is allowed. Prior YNDX history crosses a business transition. See the [resumption announcement](https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-group-announces-planned-resumption-of-trading-on-nasdaq-and-provides-investor-update).

Q3 financial materials describe USD presentation and restated prior currency figures. ARR is a monthly run-rate calculation, not recognized annual revenue. December financing agreements were subject to closing conditions; the answer must not assert cash had already arrived.

### IREN

The [2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000187884825000063/iren-20250630.htm) describes loss of foreign-private-issuer qualification and US GAAP comparatives. The [original FY2024 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000162828024038677/iren-20240630.htm) and [March 2025 amendment](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000187884825000020/iren-20240630.htm) are distinct vintages. Do not infer a precise form-transition boundary from one status date; submissions still contain 6-Ks during 2025.

The November 6 update reports October electricity cost of 20,834 USD/BTC; December 6 revises it to 18,896 after congestion revenue rights credits. These answer different version-specific questions. Installed hashrate differs from monthly average; hardware profit differs from full net profit.

### AST SpaceMobile

Distinguish consolidated loss from loss attributable to common stockholders, quarterly from annual columns, and cash equivalents from restricted cash. Adjusted operating expenses are non-GAAP.

March 3's joint-venture announcement precedes afternoon results. A shared event cluster cannot make later facts available earlier. October warrant completion traces to an [August-initiated redemption](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1780312/000149315224039227/ex99-1.htm), so it is not a new October first-disclosure event.

## 5. Learning notes

- **RAG:** Retrieve by company, period and version as well as meaning. Similar text from the wrong period can support a convincing false answer.
- **Structured calculations:** Preserve typed numbers/units and source rows; calculate with ordinary deterministic code.
- **Harnesses:** These files define inputs, grading references and scoring. A runnable answerer/retriever harness is a later slice; present checks validate data integrity only.
- **Agents:** Bounded researchers, one integrating writer, fresh falsification reviewer. Agreement is evidence to check, not proof.
- **LangGraph/LangChain:** Possible tools for state, routing and retrieval after pipeline needs are known.
- **MCP:** A possible interface to retrieval/calculation tools. It does not fix missing history, versions or usage rights.
- **Forecasting:** More horizons add labeling and leakage requirements; correlated cases do not create independent statistical evidence.
